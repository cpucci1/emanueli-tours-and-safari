Your driver-guide

Not an agency. Just Emanuele, and the road ahead.

Born and raised near Arusha, I've spent my career reading the bush — where the herds move, when the crater mist lifts, which track the big cats favour at dawn. Every itinerary on this site is one I've driven myself, over and over.

You won't be handed off between offices or drivers. From the moment I collect you at the airport to the moment I drop you off, I'm your guide, your driver and your translator — fluent in English, French and Swahili.
